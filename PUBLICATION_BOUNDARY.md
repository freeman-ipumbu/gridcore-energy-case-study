# Public case-study boundary

This repository presents the GridCore Energy identity and visitor experience. It is a portfolio record, separate from the private production website repository.

## Included

- The site-first design concept and visitor journey
- Public-facing identity artwork and the visual system
- Campaign artwork and a conceptual experience diagram
- A description of the website's public features and engineering approach
- The Cloudflare Pages destination

## Kept private

- Production HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Worker source
- Cloudflare account configuration, API keys, deployment credentials and secrets
- Enquiry records, contact details submitted by visitors and campaign event data
- Staff access methods, internal operations and unpublished client materials

The case study describes the delivered interface and architecture. It does not present unverified conversion, revenue, installation or customer results. The artwork shown here may be viewed and linked; this repository grants no right to reuse GridCore's identity or the case-study visuals as a commercial template.
